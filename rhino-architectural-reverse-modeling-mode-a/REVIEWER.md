# Independent Architectural Review

Use for a specifically assigned question or a requested delivery review. Read
`SKILL.md` for the user's fixed detailed-model objective and applicable scope.
Choose your own methods and evidence; the topics below are decision aids, not
a checklist for every review. Select useful review moments and scope under
SKILL.md; control geometry, assembled components and delivery are opportunities,
not three mandatory rounds. Do not postpone consequential form review until after
dependent detailing. There are no required report templates or status codes.
Early reference analysis is distinct from model review. Do not modify the model during a read-only
review. Raw user constraints and references take precedence over the modeler's
account of what supposedly passed. Read those inputs independently and inspect
actual geometry or views derived from it; the modeler's summary substitutes for
neither. For oral-only tasks, use the user's conditions and intent.

## Decide from the actual result

- For form: inspect the original reference and comparable model view, both whole
  and at defining contours, openings and volume relationships. A bounding box,
  matching endpoints, object count or aggregate score cannot establish form.
  Consider whether the geometric representation can express the observed
  variation; do not assume the initial form is sound and only review details.
  Distinguish geometry from projection, occlusion and lighting. Identify visible
  local contradictions; see `references/form-verification.md`
  when a more discriminating comparison is needed.
- For photo viewpoint: check projection, vertical correction and crop using
  features at different positions and depths, not only the silhouette. Check the
  actual output against the reference and its saved view; camera changes must
  not conceal geometry discrepancies.
- For dimensions: query geometry against user conditions and supported readings.
  Re-derive disputed quantities with useful independent evidence when available.
  Do not demand a second photograph to validate a user-given dimension, or treat
  a hidden inferred depth as a verified fact.
- For assembly: check real openings, depth, thickness, support, junctions and
  repeated systems in relevant oblique/section views. Check component positions,
  counts, sections and levels against the sources, and circulation endpoints,
  ground contact and spatial continuity. Source fidelity and construction
  plausibility are separate judgments; inference must not override known facts.
  Apply the building's actual
  type and agreed interior/site scope, not a universal high-rise template.
- For editability: inspect names, layers/collections, instances and accessible
  controls in the native file. Choose representative edits in a disposable copy
  if static inspection cannot establish practical editability.
- For materials and scene: inspect actual assignment, optical/surface properties,
  pattern existence, scale/mapping and promised context. Scene richness cannot
  excuse building errors. Use `references/material-review-display.md` as needed.
- For delivery: inspect the saved native file, resources and actual rendered
  images. Every task needs a rendered primary view and at least one complementary
  rendered view, with both recoverable cameras/views in the model. For photo tasks
  the primary matches the main reference; for oral-only tasks it expresses the
  design intent. Check that supplementary views reveal useful spatial information.
  A saved camera, clay capture or generated picture alone is incomplete.

For a targeted review, conclude only about the assigned scope. A delivery review
covers every applicable outcome dimension in SKILL.md; do not let one strong
dimension compensate for another failure. Unknown or uninspected evidence stays
unverified. Use proportional means to obtain it; missing a preferred form or tool
does not itself invalidate otherwise adequate direct evidence.

Methods and order are flexible; applicable outcome coverage is required. Reuse
findings that remain valid for the current model, and inspect gaps or affected
parts. The main modeler retains responsibility for complete coverage: silence
from a targeted reviewer is not evidence that other parts are correct. No separate
tool call or written checklist is required for each dimension.

## Report what changes the decision

Report concrete findings, the evidence needed to assess them, and their impact
or correction. Do not produce a full checklist for a targeted question. Link useful images or name the
affected controls/objects. Severity reflects effect on the user's model, not
whether a checkbox is present. Clearly visible defining-form errors prevent
acceptance even if all computed metrics pass.

Distinguish acceptable stated inference from a contradiction or missing promised
work. The modeler may correct a finding or rebut it with evidence; unsupported
agreement or dismissal does not resolve it. A corrected issue needs relevant new
evidence; do not repeat every check
after an unrelated small change. Report a clean finding only for what was checked.
